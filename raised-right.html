◆ Our Beef ◆
Grass-Finished No Hormones USDA Inspected

Our Standard. Our Name On It.

Our beef comes from a family-owned Georgia ranch whose cattle-raising practices we're proud to be a part of, resulting in a leaner, cleaner, and more nutrient-dense cut of beef, raised the way cattle are meant to live.

Richer Nutrition
Grass-finished beef contains significantly more omega-3s, CLA, and vitamins than grain-fed. Real pasture makes the difference on your plate.
Nothing Hidden
No growth hormones. No unnecessary antibiotics. What you see is everything that's in it — clean protein, straight from Georgia pasture.
Flavor You Can Taste
Cattle that move develop real marbling from muscle use, not feedlot weight gain. That's the depth of flavor in every cut we carry.
Land & Soil
Pasture-Only, Start to Finish
Open forage from birth to harvest. No grain, no feedlots. The land is the feed.
Adaptive Paddock Rotation
The herd shifts to a fresh section every one to seven days. Each paddock rests fully before they return.
Cover Cropping
Protective crops go in the ground between grazes, rebuilding and shielding the soil for the next cycle.
Natural Nutrient Cycling
Herd movement distributes nutrients across pasture without synthetic fertilizer. The land feeds itself.
Herd & Animal Care
Water Stewardship
Managed pasture reduces runoff and holds moisture in the soil where the land needs it most.
Genetics & Herd Selection
Animals chosen for strength and eating quality. Breeding designed to improve the herd over generations.
Low-Stress Handling
Calm, deliberate care from first day to last. Animals raised without chronic stress produce cleaner beef.
Prevention-First Health
Strict protocols keep the herd clean and strong without defaulting to a cabinet full of antibiotics.
